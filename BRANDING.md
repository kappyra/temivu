# Temivu — provisional identity

Temivu (suggested pronunciation: TEH-mi-voo) is a proposed six-letter working name for this experimental egui library. It has not been registered or reserved. Selection is provisional, subject to a proper trademark and broader name clearance before any public release. The project is independent from Forui and does not imply endorsement.

## Screening, 29 October 2026

- [crates.io API, exact `temivu`](https://crates.io/api/v1/crates/temivu) returned HTTP 404 at the time of the check. This does not reserve the name.
- [GitHub repository search, `temivu in:name`](https://github.com/search?q=temivu+in%3Aname&type=repositories) returned zero repositories through the unauthenticated API. This is not a complete search of organizations, accounts, private repositories, or unindexed projects.
- Exact-phrase web searches for “temivu”, software, app and trademark produced no clear exact software brand in the returned results. Search-engine coverage is incomplete and changes over time.
- Rejected candidates: [Taviri](https://taviri.io/cloud) is an existing software product, [Orveta](https://in.linkedin.com/in/orveta-erp-09b154370) is used for ERP software, and [Velumiq](https://velumiq.de/) is a software business. Earlier screening also rejected Velori and Nuvori.
- Dedicated trademark registry searches (including USPTO, EUIPO and WIPO) were not completed. Search-engine results are not a substitute. Similar-sounding marks, regional use, and visual mark conflicts remain unverified. Do not claim legal uniqueness or trademark clearance.

## Mark and colors

The original geometric mark combines a broad T-shaped structure and a separate rounded tile, referring to reusable UI pieces. `branding/logo.svg` is the primary enclosed mark; `logo-light.svg` and `logo-dark.svg` are transparent variants for light and dark backgrounds. The primary indigo is `#4338ca`, white is `#ffffff`, and the accent cyan is `#67e8f9`. The light-surface variant uses `#312e81` and `#0891b2` for contrast. The supplied 512-pixel PNG and multi-size ICO reproduce the primary mark. Source consists of simple independently drawn geometric shapes; similarity to existing marks has not been exhaustively searched.

The SVG assets and raster derivatives are original project materials under the project MIT license. The mark is not an upstream Forui logo and does not use its assets. This experimental repository uses the provisional name. Trademark clearance, including registry searches, remains future work.
