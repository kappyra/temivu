# Asset provenance and licensing

This page records the assets committed to this repository and their terms. It is a
provenance record, not a legal audit and not a statement that every third-party
notice obligation is satisfied.

## Checked-in assets

| Category | Files | Location | Terms |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Project branding (original) | 5 | `branding/` | Project MIT license; see `BRANDING.md` |
| Reproduced font license texts | 4 | `third_party/egui-fonts/` | License and attribution texts reproduced from `epaint_default_fonts` 0.36.2; the bundled fonts remain under their own terms |
| Collected upstream license texts | 40 (including a metadata notice record for `dispatch`) | `third_party/upstream-licenses/` | License texts for cached dependency packages; provenance in `provenance.json` |
| Project-generated review baselines | 86 | `harness/visual-review/baselines/` | Project MIT license; generated by the off-screen visual-review helper |

The branding is original project work: the geometric mark and its SVG/PNG/ICO
derivatives were drawn for this project and are not derived from Forui or any other
product. `BRANDING.md` records the provisional name screening and notes that
trademark clearance, including registry searches, has not been performed.

## Known gaps

- `dispatch` 0.2.0 (a transitive macOS dependency) declares MIT in its metadata and
  on crates.io, but no license text, copyright line or notice file exists in the
  packaged crate, at its recorded upstream revision, or in current upstream
  `master`; the GitHub license API reports none. No text was fabricated. The gap is
  recorded in `third_party/upstream-licenses/dispatch/0.2.0/README.md`.
- The upstream `objc2` licensing description states that the crates are derived from
  Apple SDKs and that it is unclear whether distributing derived works is allowed.
  The packages declare MIT (core) or `Zlib OR Apache-2.0 OR MIT` (framework crates).
  This is a recorded, unresolved question, not a legal conclusion; see
  `third_party/upstream-licenses/objc2-packages.json` and upstream issue #23.
- Some packages in the locked dependency graph have no matching root-level license
  file after adding AUTHORS and CONTRIBUTORS files; texts may live elsewhere or the
  license expressions may offer alternatives. The upstream license texts collected
  in `third_party/upstream-licenses/` cover the packages checked during the port.
- The complete transitive notice bundle for the optional `demo` feature and the
  visual-review helper has not been assembled as a distributable artifact, and no
  distribution artifact has been produced yet, so distribution obligations
  (including font and asset attribution placement) have not been exercised.
- The Linux accessibility dependency chain retains three unsuppressed informational
  unmaintained notices (RUSTSEC-2026-0370/0371/0372); no maintained replacement was
  available as of November 2026. Accessibility remains enabled. See
  `docs/DEPENDENCY_ADVISORIES.md`.

These gaps are recorded rather than hidden.
